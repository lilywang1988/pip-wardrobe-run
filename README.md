# Paws Run

A small browser platformer: play as a dog or a cat, choose a breed, fur, gender and clothes, and clear three rounds (Easy, Medium, Hard). Dogs collect bones, cats collect fish; the wrong one costs a heart.

Run on Earth (grassland, ocean, blue sky), the Moon or any planet from Mercury to Neptune, each with its own scenery, gravity, music and sounds, plus the occasional meteor shower.

Play: https://lilywang1988.github.io/pip-wardrobe-run/
